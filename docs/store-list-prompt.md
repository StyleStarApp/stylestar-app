I run a personal styling app for women in the US. I have a list of shops the
app is allowed to recommend, and I want to expand it.

Please suggest **150 more US-shoppable women's fashion retailers** I do not
already have.

**Give me names only.** One per line. No descriptions, no numbering, no
commentary, no categories, nothing else. Just the shop names.

**Rules about what to include:**
- Must sell women's clothing, shoes, bags, jewellery or accessories
- Must ship in the US, and a shopper must be able to buy and keep a specific
  item from their own website
- Spread them across every price level, from genuinely affordable to luxury.
  I want a lot of affordable and mid-market ones, not mostly designer.
- Include shops that are strong in particular things: denim, dresses,
  workwear, occasion, shoes, bags, jewellery, activewear, swim, outerwear,
  lingerie, and extended sizing (plus, petite, tall, wide-width shoes)

**Do NOT include any of these:**
- Subscription styling boxes (Stitch Fix, Trunk Club, Wantable, Dia&Co,
  Fabletics or similar)
- Clothing rental services (Rent the Runway, Nuuly or similar)
- Fast fashion (Shein, Temu, Cider, Princess Polly, Meshki, Peppermayo,
  Cotton On, Fashion Nova, boohoo, Ardene or similar)
- Resale, consignment or second-hand (ThredUp, Poshmark, The RealReal,
  Depop, Vestiaire or similar)
- Marketplaces where the seller is a random third party rather than the shop
  itself
- Menswear-only or kids-only shops
