Our Story
Style Star was created by a certified image consultant with 20+ years of styling experience and a lifetime love of shopping. Let's just say, she's been shopping since before she could walk. Some of her earliest happy memories are browsing department stores with her mom.
She turned that passion into a career doing closet consultations, shopping trips, and custom boutique try-on sessions with real women. Along the way, she realized something: most women aren't looking for a makeover. They want to feel more confident, more pulled together, and more like themselves. They want to stop staring at a full closet feeling like they have nothing to wear, and start looking in the mirror thinking, "Yes, this is me!"
Style Star was built to bring that experience to every woman. A personal stylist at your fingertips that knows your style, answers your questions, and helps you shop with confidence.
The approach is rooted in the idea that style lives on a spectrum. Some women lean classic, others trendy. Some feel best in effortless simplicity, others come alive in glamour and boldness. Your style is a melody, a beautiful combination of notes that make you you.
Our founder will tell you her favorite moments are when a client tries something on and everything clicks. You can feel the energy change. That's the magic: when your clothes match who you actually are, not what a trend or a brand tells you to be. That's when we shine brighter being ourselves.
Sometimes the smallest adjustment changes everything. One notch more polished. One notch more relaxed. One notch more bold. The goal is alignment. Thoughtful guidance designed to help you become your own style star.
Here's something most people don't realize about shopping: 99% of what's in any given store is going to be a no. That's not failure. That's fashion. Real style is in the editing, the refining, and knowing what deserves a yes. Women already have great instincts about their personal style. They just need help defining it and building the wardrobe to match. That's what Style Star does. It brings your own intuition into sharper focus and makes shopping for the real you fun and easy.
We believe your style is one of the most powerful ways you shine your light to the world. Finding outfits and pieces that suit your unique preferences isn't just shopping. It's how we build a wardrobe with meaning. It's how we make our own stars align.
New clothes are like fresh flowers, enjoy them now.

Questions We Hear Often
What is Style Star?
Style Star combines the insight of a personal stylist with the convenience of modern technology. Every tool is here to enhance your style: the quiz helps us understand your style's nuances, styling advice and photo feedback are there whenever you need them, and curated shopping links take you straight to the good stuff, making it all fun and easy. We turn the overwhelm of endless choices into the joy of finding what's truly you.
What if I don't know what my style is?
That's exactly why Style Star exists. You don't need to have it all figured out. That's our job. We help you discover what suits your personality, lifestyle, and preferences, so shopping feels easier, getting dressed feels faster, and every piece you bring home brings out the best in you. Your discernment, combined with our expertise, is where the magic happens.
What makes Style Star different?
Most style advice tells everyone to wear the same trends or invest in certain brands. Style Star starts with you. Instead of asking "What's fashionable?" we ask "What feels most like you?" We're not here to tell you what's beautiful. We help you recognize what feels beautiful to you, and shine a light on exactly that.
Do I have to take the quiz?
Not at all. You're welcome to chat with your stylist, analyze a photo, or browse and shop anytime. The quiz helps us understand the nuances of your style so everything feels more personal, but it's completely optional.
What is my Style Portrait?
Your Style Portrait is a snapshot of your personal style from the quiz. It highlights your natural preferences and becomes the foundation for the guidance, shopping ideas, and advice you'll get throughout the app.
Can I share my results?
Yes, and we'd love that! After the quiz, you'll get a beautiful Style Card with your Style Signature that you can text to a friend or post on Instagram. It's a fun way to celebrate your style, and to invite the women you love to discover theirs.
Can I upload a photo for style advice?
Yes. Share an outfit or a piece you're considering, and your stylist will give you thoughtful feedback to elevate the look while keeping it true to you. Your photo is processed in real time and never saved.
Is this a real stylist or AI?
Both. Style Star was created by a certified image consultant with 20+ years of styling experience, and your AI stylist is trained on her approach and philosophy, so you get thoughtful, professional guidance anytime.
Can I retake the quiz or change my results?
Yes, as many times as you want. Your style can evolve, and your results can grow right along with it.
How do I get my results back on another device?
Just your email. You don't need to create a password or remember anything. Simply save your results with your email address and you can pick up right where you left off, on any device.
Can I add Style Star to my phone like an app?
Yes! You can add Style Star to your home screen so it opens full-screen with its own icon, just like an app. No app store needed. 📲
On an iPhone (using Safari):
  1. Open stylestar.app in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share icon at the bottom of the screen (the square with an arrow pointing up).
  3. Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen."
  4. Tap "Add" in the top right.
On an Android phone (using Chrome):
  1. Open stylestar.app in Chrome.
  2. Tap the menu (the three dots in the top right).
  3. Tap "Add to Home screen," then "Add."
Is Style Star free?
Yes, Style Star is free to use. If you shop through some of our links, we may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you, and that's what helps keep it free.
Is my information private?
Yes. We use your information only to personalize your styling experience and save your results. We never sell your information. You can read the details in our Privacy Policy.
Do you sell the clothes?
No, we don't sell anything ourselves. We point you to the stores and pieces that suit your style. In the Style Star Edit, you'll find pieces personally hand-selected by our founder, the ones she wears herself and recommends to her clients.
Is Style Star for every body, age, and budget?
Absolutely. Style is personal, and Style Star is here to celebrate yours, whatever your size, age, or budget. Beautiful style exists at every price point, and you don't need to be a fashion expert. We meet you exactly where you are.
How do I get in touch?
We'd love to hear from you. Reach us at hello@stylestar.app.
What is the heart behind Style Star?
We believe style has the power to help you shine your light, and when you feel confident in yourself, that light naturally reaches the people around you. 💫
Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains what information Style Star collects, how we use it, and your choices.

What we collect

When you save your results, we collect your email address, quiz answers, style portrait, and any preferences you share (sizes, colors, etc.). Photos shared for style analysis are processed by AI in real time and are never stored.

How we use it

We use your information to save your style profile so you can access it across devices, to personalize your shopping suggestions, and to send you occasional styling updates. We never sell or share your personal information with third parties.

Affiliate links

Some product links on Style Star are affiliate links. When you make a purchase through these links, we may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. This never influences which items we recommend.

Your choices

You can use Style Star without saving your results or providing an email. If you have saved your results and would like your data deleted, email us and we will remove it promptly.

Contact

Questions about your privacy? Reach us at hello@stylestar.app
