Our Story
Style Star was created by a certified image consultant with 20+ years of styling experience and a lifetime love of shopping. Let's just say, she's been shopping since before she could walk. Some of her earliest happy memories are browsing department stores with her mom.
She turned that passion into a career doing closet consultations, shopping trips, and custom boutique try-on sessions with real women. Along the way, she realized something: most women aren't looking for a makeover. They want to feel more confident, more pulled together, and more like themselves. They want to stop staring at a full closet feeling like they have nothing to wear, and start looking in the mirror thinking, "Yes, this is me!"
Style Star was built to bring that experience to every woman. A personal stylist in your pocket that knows your style, answers your questions, and helps you shop with confidence.
The approach is rooted in the idea that style lives on a spectrum. Some women lean classic, others trendy. Some feel best in effortless simplicity, others come alive in glamour and boldness. Your style is a melody, a beautiful combination of notes that make you you.
Our founder will tell you her favorite moments are when a client tries something on and everything clicks. You can feel the energy change. That's the magic: when your clothes match who you actually are, not what a trend or a brand tells you to be. That's when we shine brighter being ourselves.
Sometimes the smallest adjustment changes everything. One notch more polished. One notch more relaxed. One notch more bold. The goal is alignment. Thoughtful guidance designed to help you become your own style star.
Here's something most people don't realize about shopping: 99% of what's in any given store is going to be a no. That's not failure. That's fashion. Real style is in the editing, the refining, and knowing what deserves a yes. Women already have great instincts about their personal style. They just need help defining it and building the wardrobe to match. That's what Style Star does. It brings your own intuition into sharper focus and makes shopping for the real you fun and easy.
We believe your style is one of the most powerful ways you shine your light to the world. Finding outfits and pieces that suit your unique preferences isn't just shopping. It's how we build a wardrobe with meaning. It's how we make our own stars align.

Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains what information Style Star collects, how we use it, and your choices.

What we collect

When you save your results, we collect your email address, quiz answers, style portrait, and any preferences you share (sizes, colors, etc.). Photos shared for style analysis are processed by AI in real time and are never stored.

How we use it

We use your information to save your style profile so you can access it across devices, to personalize your shopping suggestions, and to send you occasional styling updates. We never sell or share your personal information with third parties.

Affiliate links

Some product links on Style Star are affiliate links. When you make a purchase through these links, we may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. This never influences which items we recommend.

Your choices

You can use Style Star without saving your results or providing an email. If you have saved your results and would like your data deleted, email us and we will remove it promptly.

Contact

Questions about your privacy? Reach us at hello@stylestar.app
