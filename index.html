My Story
I'm Catherine, a personal stylist and certified image consultant with over 20 years of styling experience and a lifetime love of shopping. Let's just say I've been shopping since before I could walk. Some of my earliest happy memories are exploring department stores with my mom and playing dress-up with my friends.
I turned that passion into a career doing closet consultations, shopping trips, and custom boutique try-on sessions with real women. Along the way, I realized something: most women aren't looking for a makeover. They want to feel more confident, more pulled together, and more like themselves. They want to stop staring at a full closet feeling like they have nothing to wear, and start looking in the mirror thinking, "Yes, this is me!"
I built Style Star to bring that experience to every woman. A personal stylist at your fingertips who really knows your style, answers your questions, and helps you find what suits you with confidence.
My approach is rooted in the idea that style lives on a spectrum. Some women lean classic, others trendy. Some feel best in effortless simplicity, others come alive in glamour and boldness. Your style is a melody, a beautiful combination of notes that make you you.
My favorite moments are when a client tries something on and everything clicks. You can feel the energy in the room change. That's the magic: when your clothes match who you actually are, not what a trend or a brand tells you to be. That's when we shine brightest, simply by being ourselves.
Sometimes the smallest adjustment changes everything. One notch more polished. One notch more relaxed. One notch more bold. The goal is alignment. I'm here with thoughtful guidance to help you become your own style star.
Here's something most people don't realize about shopping: 99% of what's in any given store is going to be a no. That's not failure. That's fashion. Real style is in the editing, the refining, and knowing what deserves a yes. You already have great instincts about your personal style. You just need help defining it and building the wardrobe to match. That's what I created Style Star to do. It brings your own intuition into sharper focus and makes shopping for the real you fun and easy. I always tell my clients that new clothes are like fresh flowers, meant to be enjoyed now, not saved for someday. There's a real lift that comes from wearing something fresh. It raises our vibration, and that energy is the most beautiful thing we can wear.
I believe your style is one of the most powerful ways you shine your light to the world. Finding the outfits and pieces that suit your unique preferences isn't just shopping. It's how we build a wardrobe with meaning. It's how we make our own stars align.
I'm so glad you're here. ⭐
With love, Catherine

Frequently Asked Questions
What is Style Star?
Style Star combines the insight and care of a real personal stylist with the brilliance of modern technology. All in one place, you can take our fun style quiz, ask questions, get styling advice and honest photo feedback, and follow shopping links straight to the good stuff, making it all fun and easy. It's designed to align your style, so you can step out and shine your light.
What if I don't know what my style is?
That's exactly why Style Star exists. You don't need to have it all figured out. That's our job. We help you discover what suits your personality, lifestyle, and preferences, so shopping feels easier, getting dressed feels faster, and every piece you bring home brings out the best in you. Your discernment, combined with our expertise, is where your style comes to life.
What makes Style Star different?
Style Star was created by me, Catherine, a personal stylist of over 20 years. It's not a faceless algorithm. It's real styling from a real stylist who genuinely cares, brought to you with expertise and love. And where most style advice pushes everyone toward the same trends and brands, I start with you. Instead of asking "What's fashionable?" I ask "What feels most like you?" I'm not here to tell you what's beautiful. I'm here to help you recognize what feels beautiful to you, and shine a light on exactly that.
Is this a real stylist or AI, and does anyone see what I share?
It's both. I'm Catherine, and I built Style Star's AI stylist to think the way I do, my approach and my philosophy, written into how it works. The AI itself is Claude, made by Anthropic. Your photos and chats are sent there to be understood in the moment, and are not used to train it.

Your chats and photos are never stored by Style Star, and no one here reads them. The details you choose to save, your name, email, sizes, colors, likes and dislikes, are kept in a secure database so your results can follow you from your phone to your laptop. I run Style Star, so I can reach that database if I need to fix something or delete your information when you ask. I don't browse it, I never sell it, and it never goes to anyone who wants to market to you.
Is my information private?
Yes. We use your details only to personalize your styling and save your results, we never sell them, and you can read exactly how we handle everything in our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
Do I have to take the quiz?
Not at all. You're welcome to chat with your stylist, analyze a photo, or browse and shop anytime. The quiz helps us understand the nuances of your style so everything feels more personal, but it's completely optional.
What is my Style Portrait?
Your Style Portrait is a snapshot of your personal style from the quiz. It highlights your natural preferences and becomes the foundation for the guidance, shopping ideas, and advice you'll get throughout the app.
How does Style Star help me build my wardrobe?
Style Star includes a list feature, called Your Wardrobe List, to help you build a complete, well-rounded closet. I took the same checklist I use in my real closet consultations and turned it into an easy list, so you can tap a star on anything you’d love to add and see at a glance what would round out your closet. Then just tap anything you’d like for shopping ideas matched to your style, or peek at What’s Trending to see what’s fresh right now.
Can I share my results?
Yes, and we'd love that! After the quiz, you'll get a beautiful keepsake to share: your Style Constellation, a star map of your signature style. Text it to a friend or post it on Instagram. It's a fun way to celebrate your style, and to invite the women you love to discover theirs.
Can I upload a photo for style advice?
Yes. Share an outfit or a piece you're considering, and your stylist will give you thoughtful feedback to elevate the look while keeping it true to you. Your photo is sent to our AI to be looked at in the moment, and Style Star never stores it. If you share it in the stylist chat, a copy stays in that conversation on your own device so you can scroll back, and clearing it is as easy as tapping "Start a fresh conversation."
Can I retake the quiz or change my results?
Yes, as many times as you want. Your style can evolve, and your results can grow right along with it.
How do I get my results back on another device?
Just your email. You don't need to create a password or remember anything. Simply save your results with your email address and you can pick up right where you left off, on any device.
Can I add Style Star to my phone like an app?
Yes! You can add Style Star to your home screen so it opens full-screen with its own icon, just like an app. No app store needed. 📲
On an iPhone (using Safari):
  1. Open stylestar.app in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share icon at the bottom of the screen (the square with an arrow pointing up).
  3. Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen."
  4. Tap "Add" in the top right.
On an Android phone (using Chrome):
  1. Open stylestar.app in Chrome.
  2. Tap the menu (the three dots in the top right).
  3. Tap "Add to Home screen," then "Add."
Is Style Star free?
Yes, Style Star is free to use. If you shop through some of our links, we may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you, and that's what helps keep it free.
Do you sell the clothes?
No, we don't sell anything ourselves. We point you to the stores and pieces that suit your style. In the Style Star Edit, you'll find pieces personally hand-selected by me, the ones I wear myself and recommend to my clients.
Is Style Star for every body, age, and budget?
Absolutely. Style is personal, and Style Star is here to celebrate yours, whatever your size, age, or budget. Beautiful style exists at every price point, and you don't need to be a fashion expert. We meet you exactly where you are.
How do I get in touch?
I'd love to hear from you. Reach me at hello@stylestar.app.
What is the heart behind Style Star?
I believe style has the power to help you shine your light, and when you feel confident in yourself, that light naturally reaches the people around you. 💫
Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains what information Style Star collects, how we use it, and your choices.

What we collect

When you save your results, we collect your name and email address, your quiz answers, your style archetypes, your style portrait and motto, any preferences you share (sizes, fit, colors, things you never want to wear, and any notes you add), and the items you save to Your Wardrobe List.

Your conversations with your stylist and any photos you share are sent to our AI provider to be understood in the moment, so you get an answer straight away. They pass through our systems to get there but are not kept: we do not store your photos or the text of your chats, and no one at Style Star reads them.

Your chat history, including any photos in it, is saved on your own device so you can pick up where you left off. Only you can see it, and you can clear it anytime by starting a fresh conversation.

How we use it

We use your information to save your style details so you can reach them from any device, to personalize your shopping suggestions, and to send you occasional styling updates. We never sell your personal information, and we never share it with anyone who wants to market to you.

Where your information goes

Style Star is run by a small number of trusted companies, and we think you should know which ones. Anthropic provides the AI (Claude) that reads your photos and chats and writes your styling advice. Supabase hosts the secure database that stores your saved results. MailerLite sends your emails and holds your name and email address. Netlify hosts the site and receives your name and email when you save. Plausible gives us simple, anonymous visitor numbers and does not use cookies or track you across other sites.

Each of them only ever handles your information so they can do that job for us, under their own security commitments. We do not send your information anywhere else.

Analytics

We use privacy-friendly analytics to understand how Style Star is used, in simple, anonymous numbers. We do not use advertising cookies or track you across other websites.

Affiliate links

Some product links on Style Star are affiliate links. When you make a purchase through these links, we may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. This never influences which items we recommend.

Your choices

You can use Style Star without saving your results or providing an email. If you have saved your results and would like them deleted, email us and we will remove them promptly, both from the database that holds your results and from our email list. You can also unsubscribe from any email we send using the link at the bottom of it.

If you live in California

California residents have the right to know what personal information we collect and how we use it (it is all set out above), the right to ask for a copy of it, the right to ask us to delete it, and the right to opt out of its sale. We do not sell personal information, and we never have, so there is nothing to opt out of. We will not treat you differently for exercising any of these rights. To make a request, email us at hello@stylestar.app and we will take care of it.

Children's privacy

Style Star is made for adults and teens. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Contact

Questions about your privacy? Reach us at hello@stylestar.app
